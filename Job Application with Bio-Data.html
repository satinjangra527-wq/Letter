Job Application Standard Format

[प्रेषक का पता / Sender's Address] (जैसे: 15, TP Nagar, Delhi)

[दिनांक / Date]

[प्राप्तकर्ता का पद और पता / Receiver's Designation & Address]
The Manager / Managing Director,
[कंपनी या संस्थान का नाम और पता - प्रश्न के अनुसार]

विषय (Subject): Application for the post of [पद का नाम जो प्रश्न में पूछा गया हो]

महोदय (Dear Sir/Madam),

This is with reference to the advertisement published in [अखबार या वेबसाइट का नाम, जैसे: The Times of India], dated [विज्ञापन की तारीख]. I am writing this letter to apply for the post of [पद का नाम] in your esteemed organization.

I have completed my [शैक्षणिक योग्यता, जैसे: Graduation / B.Com / M.P.Ed] from [यूनिवर्सिटी/संस्थान का नाम] with [प्रतिशत/श्रेणी, यदि दी हो] marks. I also possess [अनुभव के वर्ष, जैसे: 2/4/5 years] of working experience as a [पुराना पद, यदि हो] at [पिछली कंपनी का नाम].

I am dynamic, hardworking, and confident that my skills and qualifications match the requirements of the job. I am enclosing a copy of my bio-data for your perusal and kind consideration.

I would be available for an interview on any day of your convenience. If given an opportunity, I assure you that I will serve your organization with utmost dedication.

Yours faithfully,
[आपका नाम - प्रश्न के अनुसार]
